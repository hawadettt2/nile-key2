## 2. Opportunity Evidence Requirements (Frozen Contracts)

### 2.1 General Requirement

All S1–S5 frozen Business Question Contracts require Opportunity Evidence as Core, with one of two acceptable paths:

**Path A:** A source/method proven to deliver: Opportunity / Potential / Untapped Demand / Market Attractiveness

**Path B:** A deterministic composition of independent evidence:
- Demand
- Growth / Import Dynamics
- Relevant Opportunity Signal

With:
- Clear methodology
- Full provenance
- NO conversion of Trade alone into Opportunity

**Key Constraints:**
- Trade alone MUST NOT become Opportunity
- No Opportunity Score / Opportunity Quantification unless governed scoring methodology exists
- Without governed methodology: report evidence-backed indicators only
- Complementary sources do NOT close Core Sufficiency

## 5. Opportunity Evidence Terms Separation

Per Phase 4 requirements, the following are clearly distinguished:

| Term | Definition | Phase 4 Status |
|------|------------|----------------|
| **Trade Evidence** | Bilateral trade volumes, trends | Partial (UN Comtrade, chapter-level) |
| **Opportunity Evidence** | Demand gap + import dynamics + opportunity signal | Gap (all scenarios) |
| **Market Attractiveness** | Numeric score or indicator | Not Available (no governed methodology) |
| **Demand Gap** | Deterministic documented methodology | Not Available (no methodology proven) |
| **Actual Opportunity conclusion** | Synthesis of evidence into feasibility assessment | Not Available (Opportunity Evidence Gap) |

**Critical Rule:** Trade Evidence alone MUST NOT be converted to Opportunity Evidence. No unsupported inference is allowed.
