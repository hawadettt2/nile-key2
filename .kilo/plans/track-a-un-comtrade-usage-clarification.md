# Track A — UN Comtrade Usage Clarification / Confirmation

**To:** comtrade@un.org  
**Subject:** Usage Clarification Request — Institutional Internal Use of UN Comtrade Data via Preview API

---

Dear UN Statistics Division,

We are writing to request clarification and written confirmation regarding the permitted uses of UN Comtrade data accessed through the Preview API, in accordance with the UN Comtrade On-Line Usage Agreement dated 07 August 2012.

## About DEM

DEM (Digital Export Manager) is an internal institutional platform used by our company for export planning, market analysis, and internal decision support. It aggregates trade data, regulatory information, and logistics insights to assist our internal analysis processes.

## How DEM Uses UN Comtrade Data

DEM accesses the UN Comtrade Preview API programmatically to retrieve bilateral trade flow data. This data is used for:

1. **Internal planning and analysis** — trade volume trends and market dynamics for internal assessment
2. **Decision support** — evidence-based insights for internal business decisions regarding export feasibility
3. **Automated retrieval** — real-time API queries to obtain current trade statistics

## Technical Capability Verified

DEM has successfully integrated with the UN Comtrade Preview API and can retrieve HS-level bilateral trade data with full provenance metadata. Technical verification is complete.

## Clarification Requested

We seek written confirmation on the following points regarding our intended use:

1. **Automated API retrieval/downloading** — Whether programmatic access to the Preview API for institutional internal use is permitted
2. **Internal storage** — Whether storing retrieved data in our internal knowledge layer for operational use is permitted
3. **Internal user-facing exposure** — Whether displaying trade data to authorized internal users within our company is permitted
4. **Scope limitations** — Any restrictions on data volume, retention period, or internal redistribution within the organization

## License Agreement Reference

Section 5 of the UN Comtrade On-Line Usage Agreement states:
> "any copying, automated browsing or downloading, redistribution, publication, or commercial exploitation of any material contained on or otherwise made available to you on United Nations COMTRADE is strictly prohibited without the prior written permission of the United Nations."

Our use involves automated API retrieval and internal storage. We seek clarification on whether these specific mechanisms are permitted for institutional internal use, and under what conditions.

## Request

Please provide written confirmation specifying:
- Whether automated API retrieval for institutional internal use is permitted
- Whether internal storage of retrieved data for operational purposes is permitted
- Any conditions, attribution requirements, or restrictions that apply to internal institutional use
- Any limitations on data retention, volume, or internal sharing within our organization

We are available to provide additional details about DEM's architecture, data handling, and internal use context upon request.

Thank you for your consideration.
